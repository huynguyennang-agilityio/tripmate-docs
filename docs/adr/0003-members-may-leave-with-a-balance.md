# Members may leave a trip while their Balance is not zero

A Member can leave at any time, even while owing or being owed money. They become a Former member: still present in Expenses, Balances and the Settlement plan, so the group keeps seeing who owes whom. While their Balance is not zero, the Former member can still read the Expenses they took part in, their own Balance and the Settlement plan, and nothing else, so they can see what they owe and why; the access ends when their Balance reaches zero. Why: blocking departure does not recover any money and only traps people; erasing them from the ledger would break the rule that financial history is never deleted.

An Owner must hand ownership to another Member before leaving. When a Member leaves, their Votes on open Proposals are discarded and tasks assigned to them become unassigned. If they rejoin through the Invitation, they are restored as the same Member with the same Balance.

## Considered Options

- Allow leaving only at a zero Balance: rejected; the group chose not to hold people in.
- No access at all after leaving: rejected; a person who owes money could not check the amount, which works against transparent Expenses.
- A Former member records their own Settlements: rejected; a Settlement takes effect as soon as it is recorded, and a person outside the trip should not write to its money records.
